Our Story

Craftsmanship Passed
Through Generations

Saleem Furniture & Interiors was founded in 1988 by Saleem Bhatti with a singular vision: to create furniture that stands the test of time — not just in durability, but in beauty and elegance.

What began as a small workshop on Darbar Road, Hasilpur, has grown into a full-service interior solutions company trusted by thousands of families and businesses across the region.

Today, led by Saleem Bhatti and Mahad Bhatti, we continue that legacy with the same hands-on dedication that started it all — one piece at a time.
